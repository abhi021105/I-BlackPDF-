Our idea

Most PDF sites are just a server with a nice waiting room.

Upload is the slow part

Sending a 40-page contract to a server and back takes longer than the edit itself. Running pdf-lib, pdf.js, mammoth, SheetJS and JSZip directly in your browser skips that round trip entirely.

Your documents stay yours

Questions

Good to know

Is this really free, no account needed?

Yes. Every tool runs in this page using your browser's own processing power, so there's no server cost to recover — no sign-up, no watermark, no daily limit.

Will Word and Excel formatting be preserved?

Word to PDF, PDF to Word, Excel to PDF and PDF to Excel are all built for getting your content across reliably, not for pixel-perfect layout. Text and table rows carry over; complex styling, merged cells, images and precise page layout don't. PDF to Excel in particular reconstructs rows from text position, so simple tables convert cleanly but dense or multi-column layouts may need manual cleanup afterwards.

What's the largest file I can process?

There's no hard cap from BLACKPDF, but very large files (several hundred MB) are limited by your device's available memory rather than any server-side quota.

Do you store my files anywhere?

No. Files are read into your browser's memory, processed there, and handed back to you as a download. Nothing is written to a server or a database.

Does Compress actually shrink scanned PDFs?

Compress rebuilds the file's internal structure, which helps most with text-heavy PDFs. Scanned, image-heavy PDFs won't shrink much without re-encoding the images, which isn't part of this pass.

Does it work offline?

Once this page and its libraries have loaded, most tools keep working without a connection — only the very first page load needs the network.

Does OCR also stay private?

Yes. OCR runs on an in-browser engine (Tesseract) that downloads its language model as static data, then reads your images or scanned pages entirely on your device. Nothing about the file itself is sent anywhere — unlike the two AI tools above, OCR isn't an exception to the local-only rule.

Do the AI tools also stay private?

No, and we want to be upfront about that. Summarize with AI, Ask your PDF and Translate PDF send the extracted text of your document to Anthropic's Claude API in order to generate a response. Every other tool on this page stays fully local — these three are the exception, clearly labeled before you use them.

Is Redact PDF a real redaction, not just a black box on top?

It's real. Every page is re-rendered as a flattened image with the marked areas painted over before the PDF is rebuilt, so there's no text layer left underneath to copy or search out. The trade-off is a larger file and text that's no longer selectable anywhere in the document, not just the redacted parts.

What's intentionally not included?

Password protection/unlocking, PDF/A conversion, PDF repair, PDF forms, and PowerPoint conversion all need capabilities (real encryption, deep format parsing) that aren't realistic to do well entirely in a browser tab. Rather than fake them, we left them out.

Can the AI tools get things wrong?

Yes. Treat summaries and answers as a fast first pass, not a verified source — always check anything important against the original document.
